## 3. Outcomes (5 blocks, alternating)

### 3a. Say it once
Eyebrow: `ONE PLACE TO ASK`
**H2:** Say it once. The right agent does it.
Body: Tell your assistant what you need. It picks the agent for the job,
starts it on the right machine, follows up and brings back the result. You
never chase agents.

### 3b. Big jobs get a lead
Eyebrow: `PROJECTS RUN THEMSELVES`
**H2:** Big jobs get their own lead.
Body: For a bigger goal, the assistant opens a lead. The lead runs its own
helpers, keeps the plan current and reports back when it's done or stuck.

### 3c. Decide from anywhere
Eyebrow: `YOU STAY THE BOSS`
**H2:** Decide from anywhere.

### 3e. All your computers, one team

## 6. Your stuff stays yours

Eyebrow: `YOUR STUFF`
**H2:** Your machines. Your accounts. Your call.
Body: Pentacle is software you run, not a service you sign up for.

Cards:
- **Runs on your computers.** There's no Pentacle cloud. Your conversations,
  notes and files stay on your machines.
- **Your agents, your accounts.** Use the AI subscriptions you already pay
  for. Model calls go straight to your provider.
- **Nothing risky without you.** Approvals are signed by a key on your iPhone
  that only Face ID can unlock.
- **Reach it privately.** Connect from your phone over your own private
  network, not a public server.
- **Open source.** MIT licensed. Read it, change it, keep it.

Fine print: Phone alerts travel through Apple's push service. Model calls go
to the AI provider you choose. Everything else stays between your devices.

## 9. FAQ

**H2:** Questions.

1. **What is Pentacle?**
   An AI team you run on your own computers. You talk to one assistant, it hands
   work to other agents, and you follow along and decide from your phone.

2. **Is it only for coding?**
   No. Agents do research, writing, job search, business admin and code. If an
   agent can do it on a computer, Pentacle can hand it out and track it.

3. **Which AI agents does it use?**
   Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini, with the accounts you already have.
   You can mix them, and use more than one account per provider.

4. **Do I need more than one computer?**
   No. One works fine. Add a second machine when you want more room.

5. **Where does my data go?**
   It stays on your machines. Model calls go to your AI provider, and phone
   alerts go through Apple's push service. There's no Pentacle server in between.

6. **What does it cost?**
   Pentacle is free and open source. You pay your AI providers as usual.

7. **How do I install it?**
   Give our setup guide to the coding agent you already use, or follow the four
   manual steps. It runs on macOS and Linux, and on Windows through WSL. The
   iPhone app is built from source today; App Store install is coming.

8. **What do I still have to do myself?**
   Decide. Agents ask before anything risky, and things like sending an
   application or approving a change wait for you.
