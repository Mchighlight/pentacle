FAQ

Questions.

What is Pentacle?

An AI team you run on your own computers. You talk to one assistant, it hands work to other agents, and you follow along and decide from your phone.

Is it only for coding?

No. Agents do research, writing, job search, business admin and code. If an agent can do it on a computer, Pentacle can hand it out and track it.

Which AI agents does it use?

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini, with the accounts you already have. You can mix them, and use more than one account per provider.

Do I need more than one computer?

No. One works fine. Add a second machine when you want more room.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your machines. Model calls go to your AI provider, and phone alerts go through Apple's push service. There's no Pentacle server in between.

What does it cost?

Pentacle is free and open source. You pay your AI providers as usual.

How do I install it?

Give our setup guide to the coding agent you already use, or follow the four manual steps. It runs on macOS and Linux, and on Windows through WSL. The iPhone app is built from source today; App Store install is coming.

What do I still have to do myself?

Decide. Agents ask before anything risky, and things like sending an application or approving a change wait for you.
